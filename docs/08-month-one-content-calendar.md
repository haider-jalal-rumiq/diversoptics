## Daily Story framework

Use 3–6 frames on active days, not filler:

1. Real store/product moment.
2. One useful detail or answer.
3. Poll/question/slider when it adds insight.
4. One CTA: product link, WhatsApp, call, or directions.

Rotate:

- New/available-to-view item.
- Behind the counter.
- Product macro quiz.
- Staff pick.
- FAQ answer.
- Real review/repost with permission.
- F-11 location/hours.
- Tomorrow’s shoot or content preview.

## Platform adaptation

- **Instagram Reel:** polished 9:16, strong cover, concise caption, product/catalog link where supported.
- **TikTok:** faster and more conversational opening, staff voice, native text/audio, direct comment prompt.
- **Facebook:** explain local/store context, include call/directions/WhatsApp, use albums for multiple product photos.
- **Google Business Profile:** truthful local update with one clean image and direct store/product relevance.
- **WhatsApp Status:** 7–15 second cuts, current availability, simple reply prompt.
