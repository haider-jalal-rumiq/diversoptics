# Social media and local discovery strategy

Status: launch framework. It becomes a dated monthly plan after the client confirms inventory, offers, access, and production capacity.

## Business objective

Use social content to create local awareness and product desire, prove credibility, answer common buying questions, and move qualified people to WhatsApp or the F-11 store.

Social is not a separate aesthetic project. Each post should connect to an approved product, service, guide, store proof point, or campaign in the same brand system as the website.

## First 90 days

### Month 1 — establish

- Secure account ownership and complete profiles.
- Introduce the store, categories, people, and service process.
- Create a consistent visual and voice baseline.
- Publish enough varied content to learn which products, hooks, and formats attract qualified attention.

### Month 2 — educate and prove

- Expand frame/lens/watch/pen guidance.
- Show real product details, packaging, fitting, and store interactions.
- Introduce real customer reviews/UGC with permission.
- Connect high-performing posts to guides and product pages.

### Month 3 — convert and refine

- Build themed drops, gift edits, and local/seasonal campaigns.
- Retest winning hooks and products with new creative.
- Improve WhatsApp scripts and profile links using inquiry quality data.
- Consider a small paid test only after organic creative and tracking are reliable.

## Platform roles

| Platform | Primary role | Best recurring formats | Main CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Visual showroom, trust, saves/shares, DMs | Reels, 4:5 product portraits, educational carousels, Stories | Ask on WhatsApp / visit F-11 |
| TikTok | Discovery, human expertise, product detail, cultural relevance | Native 9:16 demos, staff picks, comparisons, myths, quick styling | Comment/DM, then WhatsApp/profile |
| Facebook | Local reach, older buyers, family/gift audiences, store updates | Adapted Reels, albums, local posts, reviews, offers/events | Call, directions, WhatsApp |
| Google Business Profile | Local intent and trust | Store/product photos, updates, offers, review replies, accurate details | Directions, call, website |
| WhatsApp Business | Qualified conversation and catalog support | Greeting, quick replies, labels, catalog links if maintained | Consultation / store visit / close sale |

Do not copy-paste the identical caption and crop everywhere. Reuse the content idea and source assets; adapt framing, hook, length, and CTA to the platform.

## Profile setup checklist

### Ownership and security

- Client owns recovery email, phone, and business-manager/admin access.
- Project operator receives role-based access, not the owner’s password.
- Enable two-factor authentication and record recovery ownership.
- Remove former agencies/staff promptly.

### Identity

- Approved avatar mark that remains legible at small size.
- Consistent handle/display name where available.
- One-sentence category/location proposition.
- Exact F-11 location and verified hours where platform supports them.
- Profile link to the website or a small approved link hub; avoid an unbranded link list if the website can route the task.
- WhatsApp, call, and directions actions configured and tested.

## Content pillars

| Pillar | Target share | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Product desire and detail | 30% | Make the assortment memorable | Macro reel, staff pick, new arrival, wrist/face/desk styling |
| Expert guidance | 25% | Reduce confusion and earn saves | Frame measurements, lens terms, watch sizing, pen selection |
| Trust and proof | 20% | Address authenticity and service anxiety | Packaging/model details, supplier proof, real policy, fitting process, reviews |
| F-11 people and place | 15% | Create local familiarity and store visits | Store tour, directions, team, behind the counter, customer moment with permission |
| Offer and conversion | 10% | Prompt timely action | Confirmed drop, gift edit, real offer, shortlist/WhatsApp demo |

Every four-week calendar should include all categories. Eyewear can lead because of the business name and visual appeal, but watches and writing instruments need deliberate recurring slots.

## Sustainable launch cadence

Recommended starting point for a small team:

- Instagram: 4 anchor feed posts/week, including 2 Reels; Stories on 5–6 days/week.
- TikTok: 3 native videos/week, often adapted from the same shoot with a different opening/edit.
- Facebook: 3 adapted posts/week plus current store information.
- Google Business Profile: 1 update/week and 3+ truthful new photos/month; respond to reviews.

This is a production target, not an algorithm promise. If resources are limited, maintain 3 strong anchor posts/week and daily engagement before posting low-quality filler.

## Content architecture

### Hero content

One monthly campaign or category story:

- 20–40 second flagship Reel/TikTok.
- 4:5 key visual.
- 5–7 slide carousel.
- Homepage/collection banner.
- Story frames and WhatsApp Status cuts.

### Hub content

Weekly recurring series:

- `Diverso Detail` — one material, hinge, dial, nib, or lens detail.
- `Ask the Counter` — one real customer question answered by staff.
- `F-11 Edit` — three curated items for a style, use, or budget.
- `How It Fits` — sizing, styling, and practical use.
- `Proof, Not Hype` — policies, packaging, model codes, or service process.

### Help content

Evergreen searchable answers repurposed from website guides. Lens/health-adjacent topics require qualified review and conservative language.

## Creative rules

- Hook in the first second with product movement/detail, a direct question, or the result.
- Show the real item large enough to inspect.
- Use 9:16 full-screen video for Reels/TikTok; design important content inside safe zones.
- Add accurate subtitles and a deliberate cover frame.
- Keep most short videos 12–35 seconds until retention data supports longer formats.
- Use audio intentionally; do not chase a trend that weakens product credibility or rights compliance.
- One post, one idea, one CTA.
- Price appears only when current and approved. “DM for price” should not replace a usable catalog state.
- Do not say `limited`, `selling fast`, `original`, `authorized`, or `best` without evidence.

## Caption structure

```text
[Specific hook or customer question]

[1–3 useful details: benefit -> proof/specification -> who it suits]

[Store/service context]

[One CTA]
```

Example framework, not publishable product copy:

```text
The detail you notice after the first glance.

[Model] pairs a [verified material/shape] with [verified feature]. It suits someone looking for [practical style/use case], without [honest tradeoff if relevant].

Available to view at Diverso Optics, F-11 Markaz.

Send “MODEL [SKU]” on WhatsApp for current availability.
```

Hashtags should be relevant and limited; rotate category, brand, style, and local terms. Do not use broad spam tags as a strategy.

## Community and response system

Daily, within business capacity:

- Reply to comments and DMs with a useful answer, not only an emoji.
- Move product/price/availability questions to the exact product link or WhatsApp quick reply.
- Engage genuinely with local fashion, university, business, wedding, photography, automotive, and lifestyle communities where relevant.
- Record repeated questions as future content and website FAQ inputs.
- Ask permission before reposting a customer image; retain the approval.

Create WhatsApp/DM quick replies for location, hours, availability, lens consultation, delivery, warranty/returns, and product inquiry. Every reply must use confirmed facts.

## Local discovery

- Claim/verify Google Business Profile and keep name/address/phone/hours consistent with the website.
- Upload real exterior, interior, product, and staff-at-work images. Google recommends truthful, well-lit images with minimal alteration. [Google photo guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6123536)
- Request honest reviews after real purchases/service; never incentivize only positive reviews.
- Reply to reviews professionally and avoid disclosing customer details.
- Publish weekly store/product updates when the feature is available.
- Link the website store page and track directions/calls where possible.

## Measurement

### Weekly

- Posts delivered vs plan.
- Reach to non-followers.
- Reel/TikTok 3-second hold, average watch time, completion rate.
- Saves, shares, comments, profile visits.
- Website/WhatsApp clicks by UTM and product.
- Repeated questions and stock interest.

### Monthly

- Qualified inquiries and category mix.
- Inquiries that became store visits or sales.
- Top three and bottom three pieces with a reasoned hypothesis.
- Best hook, format, product category, CTA, and posting window.
- Follower quality and local audience share, not only total growth.
- Google profile calls, directions, website clicks, and review trend.

### Experiment rules

- Maintain a simple hypothesis log.
- Test one major variable when possible.
- Recut a strong idea with a new hook before declaring the topic exhausted.
- Do not change the brand system based on one post.
- Replace generic timing advice with account Insights after the first month.

## Paid media gate

Do not spend on ads until:

- Profiles, website/WhatsApp handoff, and tracking are working.
- At least 3–5 organic creatives have shown qualified interest.
- Target geography and business capacity are confirmed.
- The client approves budget, offer, policy, and lead response owner.

Then start with local click-to-WhatsApp or store-awareness tests using proven creative, not a large catalog campaign.

## Monthly production loop

1. **Review:** previous results, inquiries, inventory, and business calendar.
2. **Plan:** one hero story, weekly series, categories, offers, and experiments.
3. **Prepare:** product list, shot list, scripts, props, staff/talent, approvals.
4. **Shoot:** batch web and social assets from the same real products.
5. **Edit:** platform versions, covers, captions, alt text, subtitles, UTMs.
6. **Approve:** factual/brand/client review.
7. **Publish and engage:** maintain response SLA.
8. **Report:** connect content to qualified inquiry and store outcomes.
