# Two brand directions for client review

Status: Golden Orbit selected on 2026-08-21. Optical Atelier is archived as an unselected concept.  
The active source of truth is `brand-guidelines.md`.

## Shared strategic foundation

Both directions express the same business position:

> A curated F-11 destination for distinctive eyewear, timepieces, and writing instruments, supported by practical personal guidance.

Both use:

- Product truth over empty luxury language.
- A confident editorial layout with generous whitespace.
- Real macro photography and human service moments.
- Accessible contrast and quiet, purposeful motion.
- An `O`/orbit/aperture motif derived from the supplied logo.
- A system that can work on storefront, website, packaging, and social media.

## Current logo assessment

Strengths:

- Recognizable handwritten character.
- Yellow is memorable in a category dominated by black, white, navy, and gold.
- The circular `O` can become a useful lens/orbit/clock motif across all categories.

Constraints:

- Only JPEG source files are present; no vector, icon, stacked lockup, monochrome, or small-size version.
- The offset shadow reduces clarity at small sizes and can feel informal for high-value products.
- The wordmark is wide and difficult to use as a social avatar or favicon.
- The yellow varies between the two compressed files, so a canonical color must be chosen.

The logo should be redrawn as vector artwork after the direction is chosen. Redraw means preserving recognizable equity while correcting geometry, spacing, small-size behavior, and production variants—not silently replacing the brand.

## Direction A — Golden Orbit

### Idea

Evolve the existing yellow identity into a warm, contemporary showroom. The handwritten energy remains, but the logo is simplified and the circular `O` becomes a precise orbit that can frame product details, animate category changes, and act as the avatar mark.

### Personality

Warm · expressive · approachable premium · optimistic · local

### Best for

A client who values current brand recognition, wants broad appeal, and wants the website/store to feel premium without becoming cold or overly formal.

### Working taglines

1. `Find your signature.`
2. `See well. Choose well. Wear differently.`
3. `Distinctive pieces. Personal guidance.`

Tagline 3 is the clearest launch option; final wording requires client approval.

### Palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Orbit Gold | `#FECC29` | Brand accent, selected state, small highlights, campaign motif |
| Obsidian | `#151515` | Primary text, dark surfaces, logo mono |
| Porcelain | `#F7F4ED` | Primary warm background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Product surfaces and negative space |
| Antique Brass | `#A8792A` | Decorative detail and premium material cue; not small text |
| Smoke | `#68635E` | Secondary text only where contrast passes |
| Signal Green | `#217A4B` | Availability/success state, with icon/text |
| Signal Red | `#B42318` | Error/unavailable state, with icon/text |

Usage rules:

- Gold is an accent, not a full-page background.
- Place Obsidian text on Orbit Gold; do not use white text on yellow.
- Product photography supplies most of the color. Keep UI surfaces calm.
- Use Antique Brass for rules, foils, and large decorative text only.

### Typography

- Display/editorial: `Instrument Serif` 400.
- UI and body: `Manrope` 400/500/600/700.
- Product model/SKU: Manrope 500 with restrained tracking.
- Urdu option, if later approved: test `Noto Nastaliq Urdu` in dedicated layouts; do not force it into the English scale.

### Logo evolution

- Preserve the human wordmark silhouette.
- Remove the permanent drop shadow from the primary digital logo.
- Redraw the `O` as a clean orbit/lens ring with optical corrections.
- Create full horizontal, compact stacked, orbit-only, dark mono, light mono, and one-color print variants.
- Use the original shadowed yellow JPEG only as historical/source reference.

### Photography

- Warm neutral stone or ivory backgrounds.
- Directional light with real, controlled reflections.
- Macro details: hinge, lens edge, dial, crown, nib, clip, engraving, case, and packaging.
- One human detail per campaign: hand, face crop, fitting, writing gesture, or wrist—natural rather than stock-luxury posing.
- Yellow appears as a slim set element, reflection, card edge, or graphic orbit.

### Motion and 3D

- Slow orbit reveal around one hero product.
- Gold ring tracks between categories like an aperture.
- Macro detail transitions use masked wipes and 4–8 px depth/parallax.
- Default transitions: 180–400 ms; one hero sequence may run 1.2–1.8 s.
- Reduced-motion version uses crossfades and still frames.

### Social expression

- Warm product portraits, circular crops, handwritten accent phrases used sparingly.
- Strong for behind-the-counter stories and approachable expert content.
- Recognizable yellow edge or orbit on covers without turning every tile yellow.

### Risk

If overused, yellow plus handwriting can feel mass-market or playful. The solution is disciplined spacing, premium imagery, precise type, and limited accent coverage.

## Direction B — Optical Atelier

### Idea

Position Diverso as a modern optical and objects atelier. The identity becomes cooler, more architectural, and more fashion/editorial. The existing circular idea survives as an aperture mark, but the yellow is retired from the main system or retained only as a heritage detail.

### Personality

Precise · cultured · modern · quiet luxury · design-led

### Best for

A client who is comfortable with a more significant identity evolution and wants higher-fashion positioning that can stretch naturally across eyewear, watches, and pens.

### Working taglines

1. `Objects of distinction.`
2. `Curated for how you see time and style.`
3. `Precision, personally selected.`

Tagline 3 is the clearest service-led option; tagline 1 is strongest visually.

### Palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Midnight Ink | `#0B1820` | Primary dark surface and text |
| Bone | `#F3EFE7` | Primary light background |
| Optical Petrol | `#0F5C5C` | Primary CTA, focus, links, category accent |
| Lens Ice | `#CFE6E2` | Soft panels and editorial accents |
| Burnished Copper | `#C06B3E` | Decorative material cue; large details only |
| Slate | `#637177` | Secondary text where contrast passes |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Product surfaces and reversed text |
| Signal Red | `#B42318` | Error/unavailable state, with icon/text |

Usage rules:

- Use Midnight/Bone as the dominant pair.
- Use Optical Petrol for interactive emphasis and white text only after contrast testing.
- Copper is a material accent, not the main button color.
- Lens Ice may fill calm story panels but not reduce text contrast.

### Typography

- Display/editorial: `Bodoni Moda` 500/600 for short headings only.
- UI and body: `DM Sans` 400/500/600/700.
- Product data: DM Sans with tabular numerals where useful.
- High-contrast serif text is never used below 24 px.

### Logo evolution

- Create a refined `DIVERSO` or `Diverso` wordmark with custom spacing.
- Build an aperture/orbit icon from the `O`, with a subtle reference to a watch dial and lens rim.
- Add a descriptor lockup: `OPTICS · TIMEPIECES · WRITING INSTRUMENTS` if approved.
- Produce precise mono and foil-ready versions for bags, cases, warranty cards, and signage.

### Photography

- Dark ink, smoked glass, steel, travertine, and deep green set materials.
- Harder light and crisp reflection control for a gallery feel.
- Product mostly isolated, then paired with one human/editorial image per collection.
- Crop confidently; use negative space for type rather than overlaying faces/products.

### Motion and 3D

- Aperture opens to reveal product material and specifications.
- Slow turntable for one watch, frame, or pen per campaign.
- UI motion is precise: 160–300 ms with direct easing and no bounce.
- Editorial scenes may use depth and film grain, but catalog UI remains still and fast.
- Reduced-motion version swaps sequences for still-image cuts.

### Social expression

- Gallery-like covers, crisp product macro reels, restrained type, and expert voiceover.
- Strong for collectible watches, premium pens, brand stories, and material education.
- Creates a clearer distinction from local sale-first competitors.

### Risk

The direction can feel aloof or too expensive if staff, store, and practical guidance are absent. Balance every editorial campaign with human service and accessible entry products.

## Decision scorecard

Ask the client to score each direction from 1–5.

| Criterion | Golden Orbit | Optical Atelier |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preserves current recognition | 5 | 2 |
| Feels approachable | 5 | 3 |
| Feels fashion/editorial | 3 | 5 |
| Stretches across watches/pens | 4 | 5 |
| Distinguishes from local competitors | 4 | 5 |
| Requires identity change | Low–medium | High |
| Works with existing yellow signage | Strong | Requires transition plan |

## Recommendation

Start with **Golden Orbit** if the current logo/signage has customer recognition or replacement budget is limited. Start with **Optical Atelier** if the client actively wants repositioning and can update vector logo, signage, packaging, social profiles, and store materials together.

Do not combine the palettes and typography by compromise. It is acceptable to borrow one execution detail after selection, but the chosen direction must remain coherent.

## Approval output

Record:

- Selected direction and reason.
- Approved master name/descriptor.
- Approved logo evolution scope.
- Approved palette and type.
- Approved tagline or “none.”
- Photography/motion comfort level.
- Assets/signage that must be updated.

Then replace the provisional content in `brand-guidelines.md` with the selected system and create Figma variables/design tokens.
