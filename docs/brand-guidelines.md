## Brand direction

Golden Orbit is the selected visual direction. It evolves the supplied yellow handwritten logo into a disciplined, warm and modern showroom identity. The experience is premium and editorial without becoming cold, generic or overly formal.

Core personality:

- Warm
- Expressive
- Approachable premium
- Optimistic
- Locally grounded

## Logo decision

The current `Diverso` logo remains the recognizable primary source. A cleaner vector recreation and production variants are authorized, but must preserve the recognizable word shape and circular terminal `O`.

Required working variants:

1. Primary horizontal color mark.
2. Refined digital mark without the permanent offset shadow.
3. One-color Obsidian mark.
4. Reversed mark for dark surfaces.
5. Compact orbit-only icon for favicon and social avatar.
6. SVG, PDF, transparent PNG and WebP exports.

Do not discard or overwrite the supplied originals:

## Primary palette

| Token | Hex | Intended use |
|---|---:|---|
| Orbit Gold | `#FECC29` | Brand accent, selected state, focus ring, orbit motif and primary campaign detail |
| Obsidian | `#151515` | Primary text, dark editorial surfaces and monochrome logo |
| Porcelain | `#F7F4ED` | Main warm page background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Product surfaces and clean negative space |
| Antique Brass | `#A8792A` | Decorative rules, foil/material cues and large accents |

| Signal Red | `#B42318` | Error/unavailable with text or icon |

### Color rules

- Orbit Gold is an accent, not a full-page background.
- Use Obsidian text on Orbit Gold. Do not use white text on yellow.
- Obsidian and Porcelain are the dominant editorial pair.
- Product photography provides most of the page color.
- Antique Brass is decorative and must not be used for small functional text.
- Availability and error states always include a label or icon; color alone is insufficient.
- Interactive colors and focus states must meet WCAG AA contrast in their actual context.

## Typography

- Display and editorial: `Instrument Serif` Regular; Italic only for short accents.
- UI, navigation and body: `Manrope` Regular, Medium, SemiBold and Bold.
- Product models and SKU: Manrope Medium with restrained tracking.
- Functional body copy: 16 px minimum.
- Functional labels and captions: 14 px minimum.
- Large display typography can be expressive but never obscure product names, filters or calls to action.

Both approved families are available in the connected Figma environment.

## Layout language

- Alternate cinematic Obsidian sections with calm Porcelain catalog surfaces.
- Use generous whitespace, hairline rules and precise alignment.
- The orbit/lens circle is the main graphic device. It may frame imagery, indicate selection, connect categories or reveal a product.
- Product cards stay clean and rectangular; avoid excessive pills, glass panels and decorative gradients.
- Mobile is the primary decision context. Main controls use at least 44 × 44 px targets where practical.

## Photography and product truth

- Use real product photography for catalog cards and product-detail views.
- Favor warm stone, ivory, soft black and controlled reflections.
- Capture macro details such as hinges, lens edges, dials, crowns, clips and nibs.
- AI-generated environments may support campaign storytelling but must never alter the product's shape, markings, color or material.
- Do not use third-party brand logos or campaign imagery until inventory and usage rights are confirmed.

## Motion and 3D

- The signature interaction is a single optional orbit/lens hero scene, not WebGL on every card.
- The semantic headline, product image and WhatsApp action render before the 3D enhancement loads.
- Default UI motion: 180–400 ms. One hero sequence may run 1.2–1.8 s.
- Prefer transform, opacity, masks and restrained depth.
- Cap device pixel ratio, pause offscreen rendering and render on demand where possible.
- Mobile/low-power devices may receive a short optimized video or layered still instead of live 3D.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` receives static posters and crossfades without losing content or navigation.
- No mandatory intro, scroll hijacking or animation that blocks the WhatsApp action.

## Voice

Curated, knowledgeable, human, locally grounded and premium without pretension.

Writing pattern:

1. Lead with the customer benefit.
2. Explain the useful specification.
3. Offer a clear human next step.

Avoid unsupported luxury, authenticity, warranty, medical, stock, price or urgency claims.

## Working tagline

This remains a working line until the client explicitly approves the wording.
