# Spacing & shape

Base unit **4px**. Radius **3px** — tighter than fashion (6px) and hospitality (12px); closer to automotive precision.

## Scale

`--space-1` 4px · `--space-2` 8px · `--space-3` 12px · `--space-4` 16px · `--space-5` 24px · `--space-6` 32px · `--space-7` 48px · `--space-8` 64px

## Shape

- `--radius: 3px` on buttons, inputs, cards, chips
- Hairline rules (`--rule: 1px`) over heavy borders
- Prefer air and alignment over decorative frames
