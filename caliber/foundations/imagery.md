# Imagery

Web photography for **watch / horology** sites. Full color. Three roles:

| Role | Site job | Subject |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Space / hero** | Homepage / campaign hero | Dress watch on marble or boutique display; room for headline overlays |
| **Material** | Craft / trust | Brushed steel, case finishing, strap grain |
| **Object / detail** | Feature / PDP | Dial, crown, hands, or movement macro |

## Do

- Controlled lighting, intentional crop, negative space for UI
- Industry-readable alone (watch without reading the logo)
- Match token ground (Ink) so heroes sit cleanly under Bone type

## Don't

- Generic lifestyle clutter, fake brand logos, neon cyber treatments
- Grayscale-by-default filters
- Competing wordmarks inside the photo frame
